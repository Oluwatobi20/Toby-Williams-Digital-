# Global Talent experience sessions

Added 25 November 2026 at `/global-talent-experience.html`.

The owner authorised adding a separate page for sharing his own Global Talent journey. The page explains personal experience and general information only. It excludes eligibility assessments, application/evidence reviews, personalised recommendations, form/letter preparation and representation. A disclaimer alone would not make individual immigration advice permissible: actual session delivery must stay within the published scope.

The IAA practice note distinguishes general information from advice connected to an individual's circumstances. Official references reviewed:

- https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/immigration-assistance
- https://www.gov.uk/global-talent
- https://www.gov.uk/find-an-immigration-adviser

The service appears separately from marketing packages, with footer links, a services-page entry, its own metadata/schema and sitemap entry. Contact links preselect the relevant enquiry type. Privacy and chat responses discourage sending immigration documents. The chatbot separates session pricing/booking from the free marketing consultation, including when generic price or booking questions are asked from the experience page.

No price, session length, availability, payment facility, external booking calendar, regulator registration or approval rate has been invented. These enquiries create no booking or payment obligation. Fees, duration, format and scope must be confirmed before booking.

Validation: HTML/schema/local-link checks on nine pages; eight distinct sitemap URLs; dedicated enquiry selection; contextual chatbot routing; existing automated mobile/desktop and chatbot browser workflows extended to cover the page. Do not describe source changes as live until production HTTP verification succeeds.
