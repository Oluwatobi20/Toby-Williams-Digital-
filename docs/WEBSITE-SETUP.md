## Before editing DNS

1. Sign in to Namecheap and record the current domain DNS settings privately.
2. Sign in to Netlify and confirm the exact site name, production URL, custom domain and deploy method.
3. Check that HTTPS works at both the root domain and the preferred www/non-www variant.
4. Do not remove or replace MX, SPF, DKIM or DMARC records while modifying website DNS: these protect business email delivery.

**Do not copy DNS credentials or private verification tokens into this repository.** DNS values and deployment settings should be checked in the providers' dashboards rather than inferred from this document.
