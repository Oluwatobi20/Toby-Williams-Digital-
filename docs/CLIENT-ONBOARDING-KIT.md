## Current offer and boundaries
- Main offer: music-first digital marketing for artists and music businesses.
- Marketing work: agree a written project scope and quote before accepting payment. Do not invent a campaign budget, deliverable count or start date.
- Separate offer: £250 Global Talent personal experience package; one-hour session, general overview of official document/evidence categories and 30 days of follow-up questions after the session, with replies within 24–48 hours.

## Enquiry handling
1. Check the business Gmail inbox and Netlify verified/spam submissions when processing enquiries. A submission in Netlify spam does not trigger a notification until marked verified.
2. Confirm whether the enquiry is marketing or Global Talent. Keep replies in the original enquiry conversation, using the submitter's email/Reply-To.
3. Send the appropriate template below. Record the enquiry in a private client tracker, never this repository.
4. Confirm scope, price, availability and any applicable cancellation terms before sending payment details.
5. Verify payment in the provider dashboard. A customer screenshot or return to a success page is not proof of payment.
6. Send the confirmed meeting invite or project start details.
7. After delivery, send a recap and track agreed follow-up dates.
